Phy.Sim is an interactive handbook for revising school and first-year university physics, from kinematics to quarks. The course content is in Russian.

• 102 live simulations: mechanics, thermodynamics, electricity, magnetism and alternating current, waves and optics, special relativity, quantum and nuclear physics.
• 48 topics in 8 sections, each opening with things to try in a simulation and five points to remember.
• 119 step-by-step derivations, every step tagged with the mathematics it uses.
• 499 problems whose answers are computed from the simulation's current parameters; a wrong answer is checked against typical slips and the slip is named.
• My path: a map of topics, a diagnostic, spaced review and a skills trainer — stored on the device only.
• A calculator with units and uncertainties; any course formula solved for any quantity.
• A lab: noisy readings, a least-squares line with uncertainties, CSV export.

No ads, no account, no network, no permissions, no Google services. Open source (GPL-3.0).
