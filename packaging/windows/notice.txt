Phy.Sim — an interactive physics textbook
=========================================


MUST READ


This application was developed by the creator Somi together with Claude.
The AI assistant wrote practically all of the course notes on its own; the
only places I stepped in were the introduction and a few other topics. The
mobile adaptation and the entire architecture of this project were likewise
built by Claude, and may well contain plenty of bugs.

Bug reports and corrections are welcome:
https://github.com/AkioArai/Phy.Sim/issues


What gets installed
-------------------

Phy.Sim runs entirely on your computer. It does not need the internet, does
not send anything anywhere, and does not collect any data. Everything you
change — settings, bookmarks, solved problems — stays on this device.

The next screen lets you choose the install folder and whether to create
shortcuts. Nothing is written outside that folder except the shortcuts you
ask for.
