Phy.Sim — an interactive physics textbook
=========================================

PLEASE READ BEFORE INSTALLING


How this program was made
-------------------------

The text of the course, the simulations and the source code of this program
were written by Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, and were then
reviewed and partly reworked by the author of the project.

That means:

  * the physics has been checked, but it has not been peer-reviewed by a
    teacher or a physicist;
  * mistakes are possible in the notes, in the problems and in the answers;
  * treat this program as a study aid, not as a source of truth. If a result
    contradicts your textbook, trust the textbook and please report the
    problem.

Bug reports and corrections are welcome:
https://github.com/AkioArai/Phy.Sim/issues


What gets installed
-------------------

Phy.Sim runs entirely on your computer. It does not need the internet, does
not send anything anywhere, and does not collect any data. Everything you
change — settings, bookmarks, solved problems — stays on this device.

The next screen lets you choose the install folder and whether to create
shortcuts. Nothing is written outside that folder except the shortcuts you
ask for.
